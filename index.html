The trade

What the bench handles

01

Resoling & heel repair

Full or half soles, rubber or leather, on dress shoes, boots and work footwear built to be resoled rather than tossed.

02

Stitching & restoration

Torn seams, split leather, and worn welts brought back with the same hand-stitched construction the shoe started with.

03

Boots & work leather

Job-site boots, dress boots and orthopedic shoes — anything built to last gets treated like it's worth saving.

04

Shine & conditioning

Polish, dye correction and leather conditioning to bring color and life back into shoes that just need care, not repair.

The shop

A trade that doesn't get replaced by an app

Longleaf sits in the Long Leaf Shopping Center off Shipyard Blvd, one of the few working cobbler shops left in Wilmington. No chain, no franchise — just a cobbler's bench, the right tools, and the patience to do the job by hand.

Customers come back for the same reason they always have: a good pair of shoes deserves a second life, and there's still someone in town who knows how to give them one.
